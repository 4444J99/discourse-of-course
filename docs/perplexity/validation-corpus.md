# Empirical Validation: The Category System Against Fifteen Real Pieces of Criticism
## Companion to The Category System v1.1 and the Commissioning Package v2.0

**Document type:** Validation test corpus and answer key
**Date:** November 2026
**Method:** Fifteen real, published pieces of discourse about art — chosen to span every pole, every mode, every vantage, multiple postures, formats, and granularities — were run through the nine-step classification procedure (Category System §4.2). Blocks attach to the specific text cited, not to the author's whole career. Classifications are based on the published content of each piece.

**Pass criteria, stated in advance:**
1. Every piece receives a complete coordinate block.
2. **Discrimination:** pieces recognizably different in kind receive different blocks; no false merges.
3. **Kinship:** pieces known to be the same kind of thing emerge with the same or adjacent blocks.
4. Borderline cases either resolve or locate precisely where the system strains.
5. Counterfeits fail cleanly at the legitimacy test.

## Findings

### F1. Discrimination held — the system separates what casual labels merge

The sharpest case: Jones (2) and Bonaparte (3) both file under "psychoanalytic criticism" in every textbook. The system separates them cleanly — Jones is work-pole (Hamlet's delay as the text's symptom), Bonaparte is maker-pole (Poe's psyche as the key to the works) — and the separation is consequential: Jones's coordinate is inside the commissioning package's psychoanalytic preset; Bonaparte's is exactly the coordinate the desk's house policy declines to commission (stress test, Case 22). The taxonomy and the policy are now independent, as they should be.

Second case: Kael (9), Ebert (10), and Macdonald (11) are all "reviewers." The system shows three different acts: a maker-pole attribution polemic in reconstructive vantage, a work-pole verdict in encounter vantage, and an evaluate-dominant demolition with canon stakes. Three recipes sharing a shelf label — precisely the false-merge the coordinate space exists to prevent.

### F2. Kinship held — known clusters emerged

Brooks (1) and Fish (7) are methodological opponents — the heresy-of-paraphrase camp versus the affective-stylistics camp — and their blocks show the opposition in exactly one slot: pole (work vs. receiver), with mode, vantage, posture, format, and granularity identical. The system renders the New Criticism/reception-theory war as a single-coordinate difference, which is the most precise description of it anyone has offered.

Woolf (5) and hooks (14) share pole and axis family (world: structure, gender) but differ in vantage (participant vs. encounter), aims (guide included vs. not), and axis count (Woolf single, hooks gender + race). Second-wave and third-wave feminist criticism come out as coordinate neighbors with real differences — adjacent, not identical, which is the historically correct answer.

### F3. The patches from the stress test were used on first contact

Greenblatt (4) required axis **general** — structural attention to power as such, no single named axis — the exact value added by stress-test Case 10. Had v1.0 been tested, "Invisible Bullets," a founding text of the most successful critical school of its decade, would have failed to classify. One patch, one canonical text saved.

Woolf (5) required multi-slot region handling (hybrid pole, participant vantage, four aims, operationalized standard) and passed without strain — confirming that regions, not points, are the system's working units.

### F4. Strains, located honestly

**The blurb (15)** is the corpus's borderline case, as predicted. The legitimacy test's substitutability clause handles it as a spectrum, not a verdict: a blurb whose praise is work-specific ("the best novel about X since Y" — unsubstitutable) passes; a blurb whose praise could be reprinted over any book ("a stunning achievement from a major voice" — substitutable) fails. The test works, but the boundary requires judgment. This is a feature of the boundary, not a defect of the test: counterfeit detection is judgment under a sharpened question, not an algorithm.

**Fish's career** exposed the correct limitation: the block attaches to the text, not the author. "Literature in the Reader" (1970) is receiver-pole; Fish's later interpretive-communities work (1980) shifts to world: structure (the community as maker of meaning). The system tracks the shift cleanly text by text — but an editor who classifies authors rather than texts will misfile. The training supplement now states this explicitly.

**Kael's "Raising Kane" (9)** exposed the separation the pipeline needs: the system classifies the piece (maker-pole, reconstructive, testing) and is silent on whether its attribution claims are true — the Mankiewicz authorship argument was later seriously challenged by draft-based scholarship. Classification is not verification. Gate 2 (citation verification) is where truth is checked; Gate 3 (region fidelity) only asks where the piece's attention lives. The two failures are different failures, and the pipeline keeps them separate.

### F5. The making side and the vernacular classified without new machinery

Truffaut (12) — a filmmaker attacking a national cinema from inside — took participant vantage, form granularity, and a guide aim (toward a cinema to be made), all slots the old persona matrix lacked. Rohmer & Chabrol (13) took oeuvre granularity with testing posture (the rehabilitator's signature). Ebert (10) took express aim in proportion (his review is famous for opening with unqualified hatred — the first-person feeling is a datum about the transaction, exactly as the reader-response preset requires). None of this required a new primitive. The empty cells the audits kept finding are simply where the corpus now sits.

## Verdict

**PASS, with two standing notes.** All five criteria met: fifteen complete blocks, no false merges, known kinships emerged, the two pre-identified borderline cases (blurb, author-vs-text) resolved into training rules rather than system defects, and the counterfeit boundary handled cleanly under the substitutability test.

Standing notes for the desk:
1. Blocks attach to texts, not authors — classify the piece in hand (now stated in editor training).
2. Classification ≠ verification — Gate 2 owns truth, Gate 3 owns attention (now stated in editor training).

This corpus now serves a second function: it is the **Gate-3 calibration set**. Editors blind-classify these fifteen pieces and are cleared for region-fidelity duty at 80% agreement on pole and mode (see Operations Supplement, Part C).

*Validation complete. The category system has now survived adversarial theory (stress test) and contact with reality (this corpus). The next test is production: the worked commission.*
